HOW TO HACK

One highlighted word is the password. Tap a word to try it.

The terminal reports likeness: how many letters sit in the exact same place as the password. It is not Wordle — wrong-place letters do not count.

Example: password HOUSE, guess HORSE → likeness 4/5.

You get a handful of tries. Fail them all and the lockout engages.

Pairs of brackets like <...> or [...] are exploits. Tap one to delete a dud word or restore your attempts.
